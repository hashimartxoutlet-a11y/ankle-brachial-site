Summary of Key Data Practices:

  • Zero Health Data Storage: Blood pressure readings entered into our calculators are processed strictly inside your web browser runtime and are never sent to a server.
  • Third-Party Advertising: This site is ad-supported and displays advertisements via Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve relevant ads.
  • Embedded YouTube Video: We embed a clinical examination demonstration video hosted by YouTube, which may set functional and behavioral cookies.
  • Web Analytics: We use Google Analytics to monitor aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic metrics.

1. Client-Side Medical Information Processing

At Ankle-Brachial (https://ankle-brachial.site), curated by Hashir Ali, we recognize the supreme sensitivity of medical data. All interactive features on this website—including the Ankle-Brachial Index (ABI) Calculator, the Toe-Brachial Index (TBI) Calculator, and the ABPI calculator—operate 100% client-side via JavaScript.

When you enter systolic pressures for your arms, ankles, or toes, these numerical entries stay completely inside your local browser memory (RAM). We do not operate a backend database for form inputs, we do not log individual physiological values, and we cannot access, view, or reconstruct your personal calculations.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

This website displays third-party advertisements served through Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-8229206721950340).

Google and its advertising partners use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. These cookies enable Google and its network to personalize advertising content, measure ad campaign performance, and prevent fraudulent click activity.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Embedded YouTube Demonstration Media

To enhance medical comprehension, we embed a clinical Doppler demonstration video produced by Stanford Medicine 25. This media player is hosted on YouTube’s domain (using privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com mode).

When you interact with or play this video, Google/YouTube may store cookies and local storage tokens on your device. These cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and are used to store viewer preferences, bandwidth settings, and video view counts. If you choose not to interact with the video player, these cookies are minimized.

4. Web Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We employ Google Analytics (Property ID: G-TTPMCFMQN5) to analyze aggregated traffic volume, popular referral sources, page view durations, and regional visitor distribution. Google Analytics uses cookies to generate statistical reports. We do not transmit personally identifiable information (PII) or health inputs to Google Analytics. IP anonymization protocols are enforced.

5. Local Storage (Browser Preferences)

We use your browser's native localStorage API solely to remember functional UI preferences, such as your chosen language edition (e.g. English, German, Spanish, French, or Italian). This functional identifier never leaves your browser and does not track you across other websites.

6. User Rights Under GDPR, CCPA, & Global Regulations

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess distinct data privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request confirmation regarding whether we process your personal data. Because we do not collect personal profiles, there is no dossier to retrieve.
  • Right to Restrict Tracking: You can configure your browser to block all cookies or notify you when a cookie is placed. Note that disabling cookies may affect ad rendering or video playback.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) / GPC: We support Global Privacy Control signals where applicable through standard browser header mechanisms.

7. Data Security Commitment

All connections to ankle-brachial.site are strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Our server configurations enforce HSTS and nosniff headers to prevent man-in-the-middle tampering.
